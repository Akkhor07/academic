Journal manuscripts

  1. Under review · Journal of Process Control

    Physics-Informed Neural Network Surrogates for Model Predictive Control of a Nonisothermal CSTR: A Multi-Seed Evaluation of Nominal Tracking and Robustness

    Md. Akkhor Hasan. Sole author.

    Compares physics-informed and black-box neural models inside an MPC loop, using a seven-seed evaluation and Monte Carlo robustness analysis.

    Read the preprint on SSRN ↗

  2. Under review · Journal of Energy Storage

    Physics-Informed Estimation of Battery Degradation Modes from Partial Discharge Windows: Identifiability, Simulation-Only Training and Validation on Two Chemistries

    Md. Akkhor Hasan. Sole author.

    Estimates battery degradation modes from partial discharge data with a model trained only on simulation, then validated on two cell chemistries.

    Read the preprint on SSRN ↗

  3. In preparation · Journal of Energy Storage

    Do Degradation-Mode Estimates Improve Battery Life Forecasts and Check-Up Design? A Test Against Capacity Alone on Three Real-Cell Datasets

    Md. Akkhor Hasan. Sole author.

    Tests whether degradation-mode estimates forecast battery life and plan check-ups better than capacity alone, on three real-cell datasets.
